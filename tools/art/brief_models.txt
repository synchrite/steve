Target look: original Xbox / early 2000s console game. Low-poly but well shaped, smooth-shaded where it matters, hand-painted or baked textures (512x512 or 1024x1024 PNG embedded in the GLB), a little ambient occlusion baked in. Original designs only, nothing copied from Fortnite or any other game.

Scale and axes: 1 unit = 1 game unit. A standing person is 8 units tall, feet at y=0, facing -Z. Export glTF binary (.glb), Y up, one file per model, under 1.5 MB each where possible. Name meshes and animations clearly.
